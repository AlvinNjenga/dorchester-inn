## Decisions and implementation notes

- **Workflow decision, 26 October 2026:** [EXTERNAL_WORKFLOWS_AND_HOSTING.md](EXTERNAL_WORKFLOWS_AND_HOSTING.md) records E01-T04. Keep original WordPress booking/contact/private-feedback forms and use explicit external handoffs plus phone/email. Preserve those original URLs; redirect proposals above apply only to a future migration after their workflows are replaced. `/leave-a-review` will link to private feedback, not unverified public review profiles. Existing map queries were extracted; exact property-result verification belongs to E08-T03. E01-T03 was marked complete at owner request; earlier audit gaps are historical findings, not newly verified facts.

- **Price conflict:** the booking form lists room options at $25/$45/$85, while room/rates pages list $15/$20/$60. E01-T03 must establish one current tariff, currency, billing interval and inclusions before prices are published. Source payment instructions also require explicit verification.
- **Content gaps have owners:** E01-T03 owns service details (including Lounge and Massage Chair), tariffs and happy-hour facts; complete standalone menus are no longer a required deliverable; E01-T04 owns review destinations, booking delivery and the map destination. About can use its existing identity copy without inventing a fuller history.
- **Redirects are proposals, not changes made:** implement permanent redirects only after content and destination sections exist. Retained same-path routes need no redirect; choose one trailing-slash convention and avoid redirect chains. Preserve validated room/date preferences where applicable.
- **Blog exception:** no individual article or numbered URL was opened deliberately, enumerated or mapped. Preserve existing article URLs during this phase; E09/E11 will map them to the chosen article routes before changing the live domain. Do not use a rule that treats every numbered URL as disposable content.
- **Migration boundary:** hosting must retain access to legal/support content and WordPress API, media and admin routes while new pages are introduced. None of this document changes production URLs or confirms business details.
